You don't have a collection problem. You have a time problem.

Kuraya takes over the repetitive work, for every movie that has a DVD-ID in the file name.

🧠 It knows the films it can identify

Reads the DVD-ID from even messy file names (it must be in the name) and asks several movie databases at once: cover, title, cast, studio and genres. Pick the best result or let it run automatically.

🗂️ Files it like a librarian

Studio, then genres, then the film, in clean folders that also make sense in Explorer, always under Windows' 255-character path limit.

🈶 English subtitles from the audio

Transcribes the Japanese speech on your own graphics card with Whisper and turns it into English. Free and private: the translation runs on your own PC with local AI (Ollama).

🎭 Knows the cast

An actress database with photos and bios from several sources, duplicates removed and aliases understood. An optional face check keeps only photos of the right person.

🔁 Finds duplicates

The same film on three drives? Kuraya finds copies by DVD-ID, file content and even sound, and keeps the best one.

🩺 Keeps itself healthy

A background guard finds missing covers, broken files and leftovers. Nothing is deleted without asking, and removed files go to a quarantine first.

📂 Local and LAN browser

Open any drive or network share and play movies straight away in Kuraya's own player.

📺 Tube browser

Search the popular tube sites inside Kuraya, play in the same player and keep favourites.

📱 Android and Windows remote

Stream your own collection to any phone, tablet or laptop in the house with the free remote app.

⚠️ Every movie needs its DVD-ID in the file name

Kuraya recognises a movie only by its DVD-ID (the product code, for example ABP-112 or SONE-804). A file with a normal text name, such as My Holiday Video.mp4, is not recognised: it is not scraped, not filed and not matched against what you already own. Put the DVD-ID in the file name and scan again. Put your movies loose, not in a folder, into the Pre-Inbox or the Inbox, new ones and also ones you already have in folders: Kuraya makes the folder itself. Site tags and suffixes in the name are fine ([HD]SONE-804-C.mkv).
