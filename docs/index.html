You don't have a collection problem. You have a time problem.

Kuraya does the work that other tools leave to you.

🧠 It knows every film

Reads the DVD-ID from even messy file names and asks several movie databases at once: cover, title, cast, studio and genres. Pick the best result or let it run automatically.

🗂️ Files it like a librarian

Studio, then genres, then the film, in clean folders that also make sense in Explorer, always under Windows' 255-character path limit.

🈶 English subtitles from the audio

Transcribes the Japanese speech on your own graphics card with Whisper and turns it into English. Free, or even better with a DeepSeek key.

🎭 Knows the cast

An actress database with photos and bios from several sources, duplicates removed and aliases understood.

🔁 Finds duplicates

The same film on three drives? Kuraya finds copies by DVD-ID, file content and even sound, and keeps the best one.

🩺 Keeps itself healthy

A background guard finds missing covers, broken files and leftovers. Nothing is deleted without asking, and removed files go to a quarantine first.

📂 Local and LAN browser

Open any drive or network share and play movies straight away in Kuraya's own player.

📺 Tube browser

Search the popular tube sites inside Kuraya, play in the same player and keep favourites.

📱 Android remote

Stream your own collection to any phone or tablet in the house with the free remote app.

🧪 Beta testers wanted

Kuraya is in beta. Try it on your own collection and tell us what breaks, what is confusing and what is missing.
