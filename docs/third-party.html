Kuraya Download

Third-party tools

Kuraya stands on the shoulders of great free software. Nothing below is installed into Windows by Kuraya: the Setup window downloads a tool into Kuraya's own Tools folder or links to its installer, and every feature tells you when a tool it needs is missing. Thank you to all these projects.

Helper tools (chosen in Setup)

Only FFmpeg is required; everything else is optional and unlocks extra features.

NameWhat Kuraya uses it forLicence
FFmpeg + FFprobeRequired. Reads video length and quality, makes thumbnails, extracts audio for subtitles, the HEVC converter.LGPL / GPL
FlareSolverr (via Docker Desktop)Recommended. Opens movie sites that are protected by Cloudflare.MIT
OllamaRecommended. Local AI on your own PC: genre sorting, title clean-up. Nothing leaves your PC.MIT
Python 3.12Recommended. Runs the helper scripts (OCR, audio fingerprints, anime-whisper).PSF
Subtitle Edit + Faster-Whisper-XXLOptional. Speech-to-text: turns the movie audio into subtitles (Whisper).GPL-3 / MIT
fpcalc (Chromaprint)Optional. Audio fingerprints — finds duplicates by their sound.LGPL
czkawkaOptional. A fast clean-up toolbox: finds look-alike duplicate videos (even re-encoded or resized), identical files, similar images, empty folders, broken files, files with the wrong extension, big files and temporary leftovers.MIT
Tesseract OCROptional. Reads the language of burned-in subtitles.Apache-2.0
VideOCROptional. Copies burned-in subtitles out of the picture into a subtitle file.MIT
